Weekly Newsletter Agent

Weekly World politics Fully autonomous

Active since April 2026

A fully autonomous weekly newsletter: every week, the agent researches the current political events around the world, condenses them into a clear, readable summary, and delivers the finished issue: researched, written, and sent without anyone touching a keyboard.

Why it’s useful: one weekly briefing sent to your email or text instead of a hundred headlines: global events, put in context. And the same architecture is flexible: an agent like this can be programmed to research any topic you choose and deliver it weekly or even daily.

Jarvis

Voice first Memory & initiative In planning

Next project

A general assistant you can simply talk to. Jarvis answers questions, looks things up, summarizes what it finds, drafts texts and emails, keeps track of calendar and open tasks, and remembers earlier conversations. Beyond that it works on its own: it operates the computer directly, runs jobs in the background while you do something else, brings up what was forgotten, suggests the next step before anyone asks, and designs and starts projects of its own. It works faster and more efficiently than doing all of it by hand.

Why it is next: the interesting part is no longer a single automated task, it is memory, initiative, and knowing when to act without being asked. An assistant that keeps context across days and does the work on its own is a much harder and much more useful system to build.
